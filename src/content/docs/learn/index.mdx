Welcome to **Photography Field Guide**, a bilingual resource for learning how your camera actually works.

You do not need to memorize every specification or buy new equipment. Start with three connected ideas:

1. **[Sensor formats](./sensor-lens/sensor-formats/)** — what full frame and APS-C mean, and how sensor size affects the image.
2. **[Equivalent focal length](./sensor-lens/equivalent-focal-length/)** — how to compare fields of view across cameras without pretending that the lens itself changes.
3. **[The exposure triangle](./exposure/exposure-triangle/)** — how aperture, shutter speed, and ISO affect brightness, depth of field, motion, and noise.

## A useful way to learn

Read one concept, then test it with your camera. Keep the subject in the same place and change only one setting at a time. This will teach you more than a list of recommended settings ever can.

Use the language menu to switch between English and 简体中文. Both versions follow the same learning path.
