Welcome to **Photography Field Guide**, a bilingual resource for understanding your camera rather than memorizing recommended settings.

Each core lesson includes a short answer, an intuitive explanation, explicit example settings, misconceptions, a controlled exercise, and sources. English and Simplified Chinese versions follow the same concepts and stable routes.

## Follow the learning path

1. **[What Is Photography?](./getting-started/what-is-photography/)** — frame a problem in terms of light, time, and intent.
2. **[How a Digital Camera Works](./getting-started/digital-camera-works/)** — follow light into the sensor and processing chain.
3. **[Full Frame, APS-C, and Sensor Formats](./sensor-lens/sensor-formats/)** — compare physical recording areas, not quality scores.
4. **[Equivalent Focal Length](./sensor-lens/equivalent-focal-length/)** — compare field of view without changing the physical lens.
5. **[Aperture and F-Numbers](./exposure/aperture/)** — connect the lens opening with light and acceptable focus.
6. **[Shutter Speed](./exposure/shutter-speed/)** — distinguish subject motion, camera shake, and focus errors.
7. **[ISO Without the Myths](./exposure/iso/)** — separate output brightness from incoming photons.
8. **[Exposure Stops](./exposure/exposure-stops/)** — calculate compensation while preserving the different trade-offs.
9. **[Depth of Field and Background Blur](./sensor-lens/depth-of-field/)** — add distance and viewing conditions to the comparison.
10. **[RAW, JPEG, HEIF, and EXIF](./image-quality/raw-jpeg-exif/)** — separate image encoding from sensor data and metadata.

[The exposure triangle](./exposure/exposure-triangle/) remains a short orientation to the three familiar settings; the individual lessons supply the necessary limits and distinctions.

## Learn by testing one variable

Read one concept, keep the subject and camera position fixed, and change only one setting. Record what changed beyond brightness. A calculator can compare numeric relationships, but it cannot decide your subject, timing, or desired interpretation.
